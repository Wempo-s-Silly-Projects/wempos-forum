# Moderation guide

This is for forum staff (admins and the owner). Everything here happens on the
moderation desk, reached from the header once you're signed in with a staff
account. Members never see these tools.

## The desk at a glance

The desk is split into sections:

- **Overview**: the desk's front page.
- **Post reports** and **Profile reports**: what members have flagged.
- **Hidden posts**: hidden posts and everything by actively banned members.
- **Bans & members** and **Appeals**: ban a member, and answer ban appeals.
- **Word filter** and **Rules**: the filter list and the house rules.
- **Images**, **Avatars**, **Profile CSS** and **GIF library**: uploads and
  profile styling that wait for approval.
- **Chat log** and **DM log**.

## Working a report

**Post reports** lists open reports, newest first. Each one shows the post and
who reported it, and you can dismiss it, hide the post, or ban the
member.

### Recent activity

Press **Recent activity** on a report to see the reported member's 10 newest
posts, including ones that are already hidden (marked "Hidden"). It also shows
how many public posts they have and whether they're currently banned. It's
read-only, so looking changes nothing. Press **Hide recent activity** to fold
it away again.

### Hide a spam run

When one member has flooded the forum, use **Hide posts** on the report. Pick
how many of their newest public posts to hide (1 to 50) and confirm. Their open
reports are closed as handled in the same step. Nothing is deleted: every
hidden post can be restored.

## Restoring hidden posts

**Hidden posts** lists what moderators have hidden, plus everything written by
members with an active ban. Each post a moderator hid has a **Restore** button
that makes it public again. Posts by a banned member come back on their own
when the ban ends or is lifted. A member's upload that is still waiting for
approval has no Restore button; it goes public through the usual approval
path.

After a restore or a bulk hide, the desk redraws and puts keyboard focus on the
page heading, so screen reader and keyboard users don't lose their place.

## What members see

- A hidden post disappears from feeds, search, profiles, the showcase gallery,
  Today's question banner and every other public list.
- A member with an active ban has their threads left out of public lists
  until the ban ends or is lifted, without anyone editing those posts.
- The desk shows post text as written, spoilers included, so you can judge
  what was actually posted.
