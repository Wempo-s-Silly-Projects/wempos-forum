# Using the forum

Welcome! This is the friendly guide to Wempo's Forum at
<https://wempos-forum.com> — for guests just looking around and for members
who want to know every trick.

## Guests: looking around

You don't need an account to read. Guests can browse every board, open
threads, search, and see profiles, the Rules page and the leaderboard.

To post, reply, react, chat or subscribe to threads, you'll need an account.

## Getting an account and signing in

Accounts live in **Wempo Accounts** at <https://accounts.wempos-forum.com>.
Press **Sign in** on the forum, sign in (or sign up) there, and you're sent
back to the forum already signed in.

Want extra protection? Turn on **two-factor sign-in** from your account page:
you'll use an authenticator app, plus a set of one-time backup codes in case
you lose your phone. Each backup code works once, and they're only shown when
you create them, so write them down.

## The boards

| Board | What goes there |
|---|---|
| General | Everyday chatter about whatever's on your mind |
| Game development | Devlogs, works in progress, and hangout builds |
| Showcase | Show off what you made. Opens with an image gallery of its threads |
| Bugs & Troubleshooting & Help | Something broke or you're stuck? Ask here |
| Suggestions | Ideas for the forum, ranked by votes (likes minus dislikes) with #1, #2... numbers. Admins can mark them resolved or declined |
| Updates | Site news from the admins. Everyone can read and reply; only admins can post |

The front page and each board have two views: **Active** (threads with recent
replies come first) and **Newest** (threads in the order they were started).
The feed shows 20 threads at a time; **Load older posts** brings the next 20.
Very long posts are folded in the feed behind **Show more**, but the thread
page always shows the whole thing.

## Writing posts and replies

- **Drafts save themselves** in your browser while you type, so a refresh
  never loses your words. When a draft comes back you'll see when it was saved
  and a **Discard draft** button.
- **Preview** shows your post exactly as it will look.
- **Formatting**: the **Formatting help** link under the box lists all the
  markup the forum understands (bold, italics, lists, quotes, links, code
  blocks and more).
- **Spoilers**: wrap text in `||double bars||` and it stays covered until a
  reader taps it. Spoilers stay hidden in every preview, too.
- **Mentions**: type `@` and a letter to pick a member. They'll get a
  notification.
- **Images**: attach up to three images (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, 10 MiB or
  less each), paste or drag them into the box, or pick from the community
  **GIF gallery**. Images are checked by the mods before they show publicly.
- **Polls**: attach a poll to a new thread. A live preview shows it as you
  type, and you'll get a warning if two options say the same thing. Voters
  can change their vote any time.
- **Quoting**: select part of a reply before pressing **Quote** to quote just
  that part.
- **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘+Enter on a Mac) posts from the box you're typing in.
- **Caps guard**: if what you wrote is mostly capital letters, a quiet note
  offers **Lower the caps**. Nothing changes unless you press it, and
  **Undo** puts your original back.
- **Editing**: you can edit your posts later; edited posts show "(edited)" and
  a public **history**. Cancelling an edit after you've changed something
  asks before throwing your changes away.

## Reading threads

- Replies you haven't seen yet get a **New** mark, with a button to jump to
  where you left off.
- A small strip above the replies shows how many people are talking, a rough
  read time, and a link to the most-liked reply.
- Long threads have **Jump to latest reply**, **Back to the first post**,
  **Jump to my reply** and **Next reply from the author** buttons.
- Replies from the thread's starter carry an **Author** chip; your own posts
  carry a **You** chip.
- **Fold** a reply (or **Fold all replies**) to shrink it to its author line.
- **Filter replies**: on threads with three or more replies, type a word to
  show only the replies that mention it.
- **Show spoilers** uncovers every spoiler in a post at once.
- **Read aloud** reads a post to you, using only voices that run on your own
  device. Spoilers and quoted text are skipped.
- Click an image to open the viewer, and step through a post's images with
  the arrow buttons or arrow keys.
- Copy a link to any post or reply in one click. Code blocks have a copy
  button too.

### Keyboard shortcuts

Once you're inside a thread, **J** and **K** move to the next and previous
post. The **Keyboard shortcuts** link beside that hint lists every key the
forum knows, including Ctrl+Enter, the image viewer's arrow keys, and Esc.

## Reactions, saving and subscribing

- **React** to posts with a like or dislike.
- **Save** posts to come back to later. Your **Saved posts** page can be
  narrowed to one board, and you can clear one board's saved posts in one go.
- **Subscribe to this thread** to get notified about new replies (unsubscribe
  the same way).

## Notifications

Mentions, replies and wall posts land in the bell. Several replies on the same
thread are grouped into one row. The Notifications page groups everything
under Today, Yesterday and Earlier, shows when each one arrived, and has an
**Unread only** checkbox.

## Chat

When you're signed in, the chat button in the bottom-right corner opens the
chat panel:

- **Lobby**: one public room for everyone.
- **Messages**: private direct messages, one-to-one or in groups of up to 10
  people. You can send images in DMs.
- Pin up to 5 chats to the top, mute a chat so it stops counting toward the
  unread badge, filter your chat list, or tick **Unread only**.
- Whoever made a group chat can rename it.
- **Message effects** (optional) make emoji-only messages big and bouncy, on
  your device only.

The **Who's Online** card in the bottom-left lists who's around, split into
Staff and Members. More detail: [Chat and Who's Online](chat-widget.md).

## Your profile

Your profile shows your avatar, bio, socials, a "Member since" date, your
recent threads, and your milestone badge. Badges go by how much you've
written (threads started plus replies):

| Badge | Contributions |
|---|---|
| New Forumer | 0+ |
| Standard forumer | 20+ |
| Big Forumer | 100+ |
| Mega Forumer | 1,000+ |
| Famous Forumer | 10,000+ |

On your own profile you also see which badge comes next and how close you are.

Other members can leave messages on your **wall**, and you can pin the ones
you like to the top. You can also style your
profile with custom CSS. New avatars and profile CSS are reviewed by the mods
before they show publicly.

## Community traditions

- **Question of the Day**: the owner posts a thread starting with "QOTD". It
  gets a QOTD chip, and the home feed shows a **Today's question** banner, with
  a **You answered** chip once you've replied.
- **Leaderboard**: the top ten contributors of all time.
- **Archive**: a public record of deleted posts and accounts.
- **Rules**: the house rules. Please read them!

## Settings

Your account settings have a **Find a setting** box and a **Jump to a section**
list. Things you can change:

- **Language**: the language menu in the header. The forum is translated into
  41 languages, each listed by its own name. Arabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu
  read right to left. If your browser is already set to one of them, the forum
  picks it up on its own. (Posts themselves aren't translated — write in
  whatever language you like.)
- **Appearance** (saved in this browser only): light or dark theme, text size
  (Default, Large, Larger), square corners, and an optional unread count in the
  browser tab title. **Reset appearance** puts these back to the defaults
  (your theme and language are left alone).
- **Who's Online**: go invisible if you'd rather not appear on the roster.
  Your messages and notifications still arrive.
- **Two-factor sign-in** and backup codes.
- **Blocks**: members you've blocked. Their posts and messages stay out of
  your view everywhere.

### Download my data

**Download my data** saves a file with what you wrote and set: your profile,
posts and replies, saved posts, followed threads, blocks, wall posts you wrote,
and the direct messages you sent. It never includes passwords, two-factor
secrets, sign-in sessions, network addresses, image files, or anything other
members wrote.

### Signing out

Signing out clears your drafts, read marks, pinned and muted chats, GIF
recents and dismissed cards from this browser. Device settings like theme,
text size and language stay.

## Staying safe

- **Block** anyone you don't want to hear from.
- **Report** posts or profiles that break the rules; the mods review every
  report.
- If you're banned, you'll see an **Appeal this ban** button. An admin reviews
  every appeal.
- Found a security bug? Please report it privately — see
  [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).
