# Using the forum

Welcome! This is the friendly guide to Wempo's Forum at
<https://wempos-forum.com>, for guests just looking around and for members
who want to know every trick.

## Guests: looking around

You don't need an account to read. Guests can browse every board, open
threads, search, and see profiles, the Rules page, the leaderboard and the
**This week** page.

To post, reply, react, chat or subscribe to threads, you'll need an account.

Prefer a feed reader? The forum has RSS feeds: `/feed.xml` for the newest
public threads, and `/c/<board>/feed.xml` for one board. Most feed readers
find them on their own when you give them the forum's address.

## Getting an account and signing in

Accounts live in **Wempo Accounts** at <https://accounts.wempos-forum.com>.
Press **Sign in** on the forum, sign in (or sign up) there, and you're sent
back to the forum already signed in.

If a friend sent you their invite link (it looks like `/join?ref=<name>`),
open it before you sign up. Your welcome card will say who invited you, and
they get a notification. Nothing about it is shown publicly.

Want extra protection? Turn on **two-factor sign-in** in your settings
(**Account & data**): you'll use an authenticator app, plus a set of one-time
backup codes in case you lose your phone. Each backup code works once, and
they're only shown when you create them, so write them down. When you set it
up you can tick **Use SHA-256** for a stronger code, but only if your app
supports it (Aegis, 2FAS, 1Password and Bitwarden do; Google Authenticator
does not); left unticked it works with every app.

## Finding your way around

The header at the top of every page has:

- the forum's name, which takes you home,
- **Browse**: Suggestions, Archive, Leaderboard, Rules and This week,
- **Saved posts**, **My posts**, **Account settings** and the notification
  bell (when you're signed in),
- **Account**: your profile, the keyboard shortcuts list and **Sign out**
  (staff also find the moderation desk here),
- the language menu.

On a phone, a bar along the bottom shows **Home**, **My posts**, **Alerts**,
your profile (or **Sign in**) and **More**. **More** holds the Browse links,
Saved posts, Settings, the keyboard shortcuts list, Sign out and the
language menu.

## The boards

| Board | What goes there |
|---|---|
| General | Everyday chatter about whatever's on your mind |
| Game development | Devlogs, works in progress, and hangout builds |
| Showcase | Show off what you made. Opens with an image gallery of its threads |
| Bugs & Troubleshooting & Help | Something broke or you're stuck? Ask here |
| Suggestions | Ideas for the forum, ranked by votes (likes minus dislikes) with #1, #2... numbers. Admins can mark them resolved or declined |
| Updates | Site news from the admins. Everyone can read and reply; only admins can post |

The front page and each board have two views: **Active** (threads with recent
replies come first) and **Newest** (threads in the order they were started).
The feed shows 20 threads at a time; **Load older posts** brings the next 20.
Very long posts are folded in the feed behind **Show more**, but the thread
page always shows the whole thing.

## Writing posts and replies

- **Drafts save themselves** in your browser while you type, so a refresh
  never loses your words. When a draft comes back you'll see when it was saved
  and a **Discard draft** button.
- **Preview** shows your post exactly as it will look.
- **Formatting**: the **Formatting help** link under the box lists all the
  markup the forum understands (bold, italics, lists, quotes, links, code
  blocks and more).
- **Spoilers**: wrap text in `||double bars||` and it stays covered until a
  reader taps it. Spoilers stay hidden in every preview, too.
- **Mentions**: type `@` and a letter to pick a member. They'll get a
  notification.
- **Images**: attach up to three images (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, 10 MiB or
  less each), paste or drag them into the box, or pick from the community
  **GIF gallery**. Images are checked by the mods before they show publicly.
- **Polls**: attach a poll to a new thread. A live preview shows it as you
  type, and you'll get a warning if two options say the same thing. Voters
  can change their vote while the poll is open. You can also pick a closing
  time, from 1 hour to 30 days; the poll shows "Closes in …" and stops taking
  votes when the time comes. The thread's author and staff can change or
  clear the closing time while the poll is open.
- **Tags**: on your own thread, **Edit tags** (in **More**, under **This
  post**) lets you add up to 3 of the tags the staff have created.
- **TL;DR**: on your own thread you can add a short summary (up to 280
  characters). It shows in a **TL;DR** box above the replies. Save an empty
  box to remove it.
- **Quoting**: select part of a reply before pressing **Quote** to quote just
  that part.
- **Ctrl+Enter** (⌘+Enter on a Mac) posts from the box you're typing in.
- **Caps guard**: if what you wrote is mostly capital letters, a quiet note
  offers **Lower the caps**. Nothing changes unless you press it, and
  **Undo** puts your original back.
- **Editing**: you can edit your posts later; edited posts show "(edited)" and
  a public **history**. The history marks the words that were added
  (underlined) and removed (struck through) in each version. Cancelling an
  edit after you've changed something asks before throwing your changes away.

## Reading threads

The first post of a thread keeps four buttons: **Like**, **Dislike**,
**Share** and **More**. **More** holds everything else, in groups:

- **Reading**: Read aloud, Show spoilers, Save as text.
- **Following**: Subscribe, Mute thread, Save post.
- **Sharing**: Copy link, and a link to the post.
- **This post**: Edit, Delete, Report and Edit tags (when they apply to you).

Menus open with their button and close with Esc, which puts you back on the
button.

Other things you'll find on a thread:

- **Tags** and the **TL;DR** sit with the first post. Each tag opens its own
  page, `/tag/<name>`, listing the threads with that tag, newest first.
- Replies you haven't seen yet get a **New** mark, with a button to jump to
  where you left off.
- A small strip above the replies shows how many people are talking, a rough
  read time, and a link to the most-liked reply.
- Long threads have **Jump to latest reply**, **Back to the first post**,
  **Jump to my reply** and **Next reply from the author** buttons. Threads
  with 8 or more replies also get a **Jump to** list of every reply, with its
  author and how long ago it was posted. Whether that list is open is
  remembered in this browser until you sign out.
- Replies from the thread's starter carry an **Author** chip; your own posts
  carry a **You** chip. Members who joined in the last 7 days carry a
  **New 👋** chip.
- **Fold** a reply (or **Fold all replies**) to shrink it to its author line.
- **Filter replies**: on threads with three or more replies, type a word to
  show only the replies that mention it.
- **Show spoilers** uncovers every spoiler in a post at once.
- **Read aloud** reads a post to you, using only voices that run on your own
  device. Spoilers and quoted text are skipped.
- Click an image to open the viewer, and step through a post's images with
  the arrow buttons or arrow keys.
- Copy a link to any post or reply in one click. Code blocks have a copy
  button too.
- **Locked threads** say "This thread is locked" and have no reply box. You
  can still read them and react.
- **Slow mode**: a thread may say "Slow mode: one reply every …". If you
  reply again too soon you're asked to wait.

### Sharing a thread

**Share** opens your device's share menu when it has one. Otherwise it opens
a small menu: copy the link, or share to X, Bluesky, Reddit or your own
Mastodon server. Only the thread's address and title go into the link, and
nothing is loaded from those sites. The Mastodon server you type is
remembered in this browser until you sign out.

### Keyboard shortcuts

Once you're inside a thread, **J** and **K** move to the next and previous
post. The **Keyboard shortcuts** link beside that hint (also in the
**Account** menu) lists every key the forum knows, including Ctrl+Enter, the
image viewer's arrow keys, and Esc.

## Reactions, saving and following

- **React** to posts with a like or dislike.
- **Save** posts to come back to later. Your **Saved posts** page can be
  narrowed to one board, and you can clear one board's saved posts in one go.
- **Folders**: sort saved posts into up to 20 private folders. Use **Add to
  folder** on a saved post, or open a folder to rename it, delete it or take
  posts out. Deleting a folder keeps its posts saved. Only you see your
  folders.
- **Subscribe to this thread** to get notified about new replies (unsubscribe
  the same way).
- **Mute thread** stops every notification that comes from that thread
  (replies, @mentions, new-thread alerts) until you unmute it. Nobody else is
  affected.
- **Follow a member** with the **Follow** button on their profile. You get a
  notification when they start a new public thread. You can follow up to 200
  members, and nobody else can see who you follow or who follows you.

## Searching

Type in the search box in the header. On the results page you can:

- narrow results to the past week, month or year, and sort by most relevant
  or newest. The choice is part of the page address, so you can share a
  filtered search;
- see your last 8 searches as chips when the search box is empty. They stay
  in this browser; **Clear recent searches** removes them, and signing out
  does too;
- **Save this search** (signed-in members): give it a name (up to 40
  characters) and it appears in your **Saved searches** list, ready to run
  again. You can keep up to 20. Only you can see them.

## Notifications

Replies, mentions, wall posts, new threads from members you follow and
friends who joined with your invite link land in the bell. Several replies
on the same thread are grouped into one row. The Notifications page groups
everything under Today, Yesterday and Earlier, shows when each one arrived,
and has an **Unread only** checkbox.

You choose which kinds can reach you under **Settings → Notifications**.
Decisions about your account, like appeal results, always arrive.

## Chat

When you're signed in, the chat button in the bottom-right corner opens the
chat panel:

- **Lobby**: one public room for everyone.
- **Messages**: private direct messages, one-to-one or in groups of up to 10
  people. You can send images in DMs.
- In a one-to-one conversation, either of you can like or dislike a message
  (press it again to take it back). Group chats don't have reactions yet.
- **Search this conversation** from the box in the chat header finds
  messages in that DM or group; pick a result to jump to it. Searches are
  not saved.
- Pin up to 5 chats to the top, mute a chat so it stops counting toward the
  unread badge, filter your chat list, or tick **Unread only**.
- Whoever made a group chat can rename it.
- **Message effects** (optional) make emoji-only messages big and bouncy, on
  your device only.

The **Who's Online** card in the bottom-left lists who's around, split into
Staff and Members. More detail: [Chat and Who's Online](chat-widget.md).

## Your profile

Your profile shows your avatar, bio, pronouns, socials, a "Member since"
date, your recent threads, and your milestone badge. Badges go by how much
you've written (threads started plus replies):

| Badge | Contributions |
|---|---|
| New Forumer | 0+ |
| Standard forumer | 20+ |
| Big Forumer | 100+ |
| Mega Forumer | 1,000+ |
| Famous Forumer | 10,000+ |

On your own profile you also see which badge comes next and how close you are.

More things you can do on your own profile:

- **Status**: up to 60 characters and one emoji, shown under your name. Pick
  when it clears (never, 1 hour, 4 hours, today or 1 week).
- **Pin to profile**: pin one of your own public threads to the top of your
  profile from your recent threads. **Unpin** removes it.

On someone else's profile, signed-in members can **Follow** them and keep
**Your private note** about them (up to 280 characters). Only you can ever see
your note: not the member, not other members, not staff.

Other members can leave messages on your **wall**, and you can pin the ones
you like to the top. You can also style your profile with custom CSS. New
avatars and profile CSS are reviewed by the mods before they show publicly.

## Community traditions

- **Question of the Day**: the owner posts a thread starting with "QOTD". It
  gets a QOTD chip, and the home feed shows a **Today's question** banner, with
  a **You answered** chip once you've replied.
- **This week** (`/week`, in **Browse**): the staff pick, the busiest threads
  and the newest Showcase builds of the last 7 days.
- **Staff pick**: staff can choose one Showcase thread as the **Staff pick ⭐**.
  It sits at the top of the Showcase gallery and on This week.
- **Newest members**: the home page lists the five newest members, so you can
  say hi.
- **Leaderboard**: the top ten contributors of all time.
- **Archive**: a public record of deleted posts and accounts.
- **Rules**: the house rules. Please read them!

## Settings

**Account settings** has its own page (`/?settings`), linked from the header
(on phones, from **More**). It has a **Find a setting** box and a **Jump to a
section** list, and four groups:

- **Appearance & reading** (saved in this browser only):
  - **Appearance**: light or dark theme, text size (Default, Large, Larger),
    square corners, and an optional unread count in the browser tab title.
    **Reset appearance** puts these back to the defaults (your theme and
    language are left alone).
  - **Reading comfort**: line spacing, always-underlined links, high
    contrast (Auto, On, Off) and **Easy-reading spacing**, which adds space
    between letters, words, lines and paragraphs.
- **Notifications**:
  - **Quiet hours**: between two hours you pick, the unread count in the tab
    title is hidden on this device. Off until you turn it on.
  - **Notification types**: one checkbox for each kind of notification
    (replies to your posts, replies in threads you subscribed to, mentions in
    posts, mentions in chat, new threads from members you follow, friends who
    joined with your invite link). Everything starts ticked.
- **People & privacy**:
  - **Blocks**: members you've blocked. Their posts and messages stay out of
    your view everywhere.
  - **Following**: the members you follow, with **Unfollow**.
  - **Who's Online**: go invisible if you'd rather not appear on the roster.
    Your messages and notifications still arrive.
- **Account & data**: change your password, two-factor sign-in, backup
  codes, signed-in devices (sign out everywhere else), **Download my data**,
  **Invite a friend** (your own `/join?ref=<you>` link) and delete your
  account.

**Language** lives in the header (on phones, at the end of **More**). The
forum is translated into 45 languages, each listed by its own name. Arabic,
Hebrew, Persian and Urdu read right to left. If your browser is already set to
one of them, the forum picks it up on its own. (Posts themselves aren't
translated, so write in whatever language you like.)

### Download my data

**Download my data** saves a file with what you wrote and set: your profile,
posts and replies, saved posts, followed threads, blocks, wall posts you wrote,
the direct messages you sent, your reactions on direct messages, your private
notes, your saved searches and the TL;DR summaries you wrote. It never
includes passwords, two-factor secrets, sign-in sessions, network addresses,
image files, or anything other members wrote (notes others keep about you
included).

### Signing out

Signing out clears your drafts, read marks, pinned and muted chats, GIF
recents, recent searches, the thread outline setting, quiet hours, your
Mastodon server and dismissed cards from this browser. Device settings like
theme, text size and language stay.

## Staying safe

- **Block** anyone you don't want to hear from.
- **Report** posts or profiles that break the rules; the mods review every
  report.
- If you're banned, you'll see an **Appeal this ban** button. An admin reviews
  every appeal.
- **Never share your password or backup codes** with anyone. Staff will
  never ask for them. Only sign in at `accounts.wempos-forum.com` (on a
  self-hosted copy, that forum's own Accounts address).
- Found a security bug? Please report it privately: see
  [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md).

## Privacy: what the forum counts

The forum keeps private visit totals so the people who run it can see how
it is used. For each day it counts how many times each kind of page was
opened (the home page, a thread, a profile, a board...) and, when you arrive
from another site, the name of that site (just the name, never the page you
came from). That's all. It does not store your IP address, your account,
your browser details or the address of the page you opened, it sets no
cookie, and it keeps no record of single visits, so nothing in these totals
can tell anyone who you are or what you read.

If your browser sends **Do Not Track** or **Global Privacy Control**, you
are not counted at all. Bots aren't counted either. Totals are deleted after
90 days. Only staff can see them.
